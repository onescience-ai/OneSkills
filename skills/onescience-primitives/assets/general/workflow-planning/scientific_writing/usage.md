# typical_workflow

1. Establish document scope, venue, design, and confidentiality boundary.
2. Build source, claim, numeric, methods, and authorship records.
3. Draft only from verified evidence.
4. Reconcile methods, results, figures, tables, and citations.
5. Complete reporting, disclosure, and human approval checks.

# usage_notes

- Keep unsupported facts in an unresolved list, not manuscript prose.
- Distinguish confirmatory, exploratory, descriptive, and post-hoc work.
- Report negative, null, adverse, and inconclusive findings when part of the study record.
- Treat automated linters as checks, not scientific certification.
